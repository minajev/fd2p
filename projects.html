We design and develop working solutions for places where failure is expensive — offshore structures, subsea systems, lifting operations, façade maintenance equipment and industrial pipe-repair solutions.

We support the full path from first idea to installation: concept development, engineering calculations, operational and fabrication documentation, quotation support, production follow-up in Estonia and on-site supervision when required.

More information of what we do on our Services page.

We are based in Tallinn, Estonia, and work with clients across Norway, Sweden, Finland and Germany.

All design work follows DNV, NORSOK and Eurocode standards.

Marine & Subsea Engineering

We develop equipment for offshore cable-installation campaigns, perform marine and subsea design work to DNV and NORSOK standards, and prepare procedures for lifting and installation operations.

Selected examples of work for our clients in this field:

  • Nexans (Norway) — cable-laying systems such as chutes, quadrants, roller tracks, trolleys and tensioner frames; vessel deck modification, design reports, production drawings, fabrication and follow-up in Estonia.
  • Fred. Olsen Windcarrier (Norway) — vessel modifications for wind-turbine installation, collision checks, lifting procedures, 3D visualisation and animation.
  • NKT (Sweden) — cable-storage and handling systems including roller tracks, trolleys and tensioner frames; design documentation and fabrication supervision.
  • Flocean (Norway) — subsea frames and mud mats, design reports, subsea operation documentation and installation procedures.
  • Layback (Norway) — tensioner and cable-drum design, analysis, fabrication drawings and production follow-up.

Civil Engineering

In civil engineering projects, our expertise covers service platforms, façade access and maintenance systems. We also develop custom exhibition and stage-decoration structures. All designs are developed in accordance with Eurocode requirements.

Selected examples of work for our clients in this field:

  • Rostek (Finland) — façade access solutions design, lifting gantries, track lines, davit cranes, production drawings.
  • Oslo Nationalmuseet (Norway) — exhibition equipment and decoration design, wooden and steel structures, analysis, production drawings.
  • Oslo Nationalteatre (Norway) — stage equipment and decoration design, wooden and steel structures, analysis, production drawings.

Industrial Engineering

We provide structural and mechanical engineering support for industrial sites during upgrades, repairs and new installations. We design steel platforms and stairways for production workshops, and GRP-profiles structures for facilities operating in harsh environments.

Selected examples of work for our clients in this field:

  • BKK Enotek (Norway) — Installation procedure, analyse and calculate the cable runs and design both permanent installation bends and temporary installation aids.
  • Glassfiber Produkter (Norway) — GRP structures design, production drawings.
  • Lango (Sweden) — electrical platforms for buss maintenance, retractable bridges, gangways, staircases, analysis, design reports, production drawings.
  • Fralex (Germany) — steam pipeline repair solutions, clamps design, design reports, production drawings.
