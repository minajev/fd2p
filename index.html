FD2P Technologies is an engineering company serving the marine, civil, and industrial sectors. We specialize in design, engineering calculations, and technical documentation, focusing on delivering reliable and practical solutions to complex engineering challenges.

From the first concept and feasibility studies to detailed design, manufacturing, and installation support, we guide our clients through every stage of the project lifecycle.
