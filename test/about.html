ABOUT US

FD2P is a team of experienced and passionate mechanical and structural engineers driven by a love for design, innovation, and problem-solving. Our story began with a simple idea: to create a place where good engineers enjoy their work, grow their expertise, and deliver results they are proud of.

We believe engineering is not only about calculations and drawings but also about responsibility and commitment. For us, delivering on time and at the highest quality is a matter of professional pride. We listen to our clients, understand their needs, and always strive to exceed expectations.

Our expertise covers marine, subsea, civil, and industrial engineering, with a special focus on steel, aluminum, and composite structures. From initial concepts and feasibility studies to detailed design, documentation, manufacturing, and on-site support, we manage the entire project lifecycle.

At FD2P, we value:

  • Technical excellence — combining proven engineering principles with modern tools like 3D modeling, FEA, and digital project control.
  • Collaboration and transparency — keeping clients fully informed at every stage.
  • Innovation and adaptability — applying cross-industry experience to find better, safer, and more efficient solutions.
  • Professional ethics — ensuring fairness, respect, and accountability in everything we do.

We see every project as an opportunity to learn, improve, and deliver engineering solutions that stand the test of time — whether it’s for offshore structures, civil facilities, or industrial equipment.
